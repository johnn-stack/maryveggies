How to Cook

Mixed Veggies

  • wash your veggies with warm salty or vinegar water
  • add your ingredients including the onions and tomatoes and the chili if preferred in your sufuria
  • add your other spices in your sufuria to your preference
  • cook the veggies on a medium heat for about 7-12 minutes and the stir

Broccoli

  • Steam or roast with olive oil and salt.
  • Add garlic and lemon for extra flavor.
  • Cook for 8–10 minutes until tender.

Carrots

  • diced into small pieces of choice.
  • added into stews to make them have a rich thick broth.
  • best eaten raw for a good source of vitamin A.

Cucumber

  • Slice and serve fresh in salads.
  • can be blended into a smoothie for a cool drink.
  • Best eaten raw for crisp texture.

Sweet Potatoes

  • Boil for the rich traditional flavour with a crispy taste.
  • Season with any spice to your pleasure.
  • Great in soups, curries, or roasting trays.

Yams

  • Peel, chop, and boil until tender.
  • Fry or roast with onions and spices.
  • Perfect with stew or sauce.

Mint & Parsley

  • Add to salads, sauces, and smoothies.
  • Use fresh in herb butters or pestos.
  • Best added at the end of cooking.
Tip: Fresh herbs and vegetables taste best when cooked lightly to preserve their natural flavor and nutrients.
